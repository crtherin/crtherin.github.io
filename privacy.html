Privacy policy

Safari Tabs add-on and Sync for Safari Tabs app · Last updated 31 October 2026

Neither the Safari Tabs add-on nor the Sync for Safari Tabs app collects, sells or shares your data. They have no analytics, no tracking and no servers of their own.

What stays in your browser

To draw the overview, the add-on reads your open tabs (titles, addresses and icons) and takes small screenshots of them for the thumbnails. These, and your settings, are kept in the browser’s local storage for the add-on. They never leave your computer, and they are removed when you uninstall the add-on.

iPhone tabs through iCloud

Only if you choose to sign in to iCloud in the add-on’s settings, it reads the tabs that the Sync for Safari Tabs app saves to your own iCloud account, and can close them there when you ask it to. This goes directly between your browser and Apple’s iCloud service (CloudKit); the data stays in your iCloud account, under Apple’s privacy policy. You can sign out at any time in the settings.

The Sync for Safari Tabs app

On your iPhone or iPad, the app’s Safari extension reads the addresses and titles of the tabs in your current Safari tab group. It saves them, with the device name, to the private database in your own iCloud account, so the add-on can show them. Private tabs are left out. The developer can’t read this database. To stop syncing, turn the extension off in Settings or delete the app.

Opening pages

Some iPhone tabs, such as Safari’s own pages, can’t be opened as they are. For those, the add-on opens a web search for the address in a new tab, using your browser’s search engine (or Google if the browser doesn’t allow it).

Store uploads

The developer uses a Google Cloud app named for Safari Tabs only to upload new versions to the Chrome Web Store. It is not used by people who install the add-on and has no access to their data.

Contact form

If you use the contact form, your name, email address and message are collected by Google Forms and stored in the developer’s Google account, under Google’s privacy policy. They are used only to reply to you.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page. For questions, use the contact form.
