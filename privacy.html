Privacy policy

Safari Tabs browser add-on · Last updated 30 October 2026

Safari Tabs does not collect, sell or share your data. It has no analytics, no tracking and no servers of its own.

What stays in your browser

To draw the overview, the add-on reads your open tabs (titles, addresses and icons) and takes small screenshots of them for the thumbnails. These, and your settings, are kept in the browser’s local storage for the add-on. They never leave your computer, and they are removed when you uninstall the add-on.

iPhone tabs through iCloud

Only if you choose to sign in to iCloud in the add-on’s settings, it reads the tabs that the Safari Tabs iPhone app saves to your own iCloud account, and can close them there when you ask it to. This goes directly between your browser and Apple’s iCloud service (CloudKit); the data stays in your iCloud account, under Apple’s privacy policy. You can sign out at any time in the settings.

Opening pages

Some iPhone tabs, such as Safari’s own pages, can’t be opened as they are. For those, the add-on opens a web search for the address in a new tab, using your browser’s search engine (or Google if the browser doesn’t allow it).

Store uploads

The developer uses a Google Cloud app named for Safari Tabs only to upload new versions to the Chrome Web Store. It is not used by people who install the add-on and has no access to their data.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page. For questions, use the support section of the add-on’s page in the store you installed it from.
