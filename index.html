Safari Tabs

A Safari-style overview of your tabs for Firefox, Zen, Chrome and Edge.

Press a shortcut or click the Safari Tabs button to see all your open tabs as a grid of live thumbnails, like the tab overview in Safari. Search them, click one to jump to it, drag to reorder, and close tabs with one click.

With the Sync for Safari Tabs app, the overview can also show the tabs in your current Safari tab group on your iPhone or iPad, through your own iCloud account.

Get it

  • Firefox and Zen: search for “Safari Tabs” on Firefox Add-ons.
  • Chrome and Edge: search for “Safari Tabs” in the Chrome Web Store.
